Your questions, answeredWhat's included with YouTube Premium?YouTube Premium offers an ad-free experience across all of YouTube, YouTube Kids and YouTube Music. Premium memberships include YouTube Music Premium, a standalone app where you can listen to over 100 million songs ad-free. Learn more.YouTube Premium also includes access to some other features:
• Picture-in-picture: Watch music content with picture-in-picture when using other apps
• Jump Ahead: Get to the content that you care about faster
• Continue watching: Resume watching where you left off, even when you switch between devices
• High-quality video: Get enhanced bitrate 1080p for extra-crisp and clear videos with lots of detail and motion
• High-quality audio: Enjoy a richer listening experience at 256 kbps when watching eligible music videos
• Smart Downloads: Get recommended videos automatically added to your downloads for offline viewing
• Queuing: Set up videos to watch next without interrupting your current watch sessionWhat's included with YouTube Premium Lite?Premium Lite benefits (background play, downloads, ad-free) available on most non-music content, excluding Shorts. Ads may appear when you search or browse. Learn more.What's the difference between Premium and Premium Lite?Premium Lite's features apply to most non-music content on YouTube. This means that you'll be able to watch your favourite podcasts and creator videos ad-free, offline and in the background.

Ad-free streaming, background play and downloads aren't available for music-focused content like official music videos, covers and dance videos. Learn more.

Premium unlocks all of YouTube and YouTube Music ad-free, offline and in the background. Plus, enjoy exclusive Premium features across all content without interruptions.How do I download videos and music?You can download and watch videos and music on your mobile device using the YouTube app, the YouTube Music app or the YouTube Kids app. You can also download videos and watch on your computer using Chrome, Edge and Opera browsers.

You can watch or listen to content offline for up to 30 days without being connected to the Internet.

Learn more about how to watch videos offlineHow do I play videos and music in the background?Background play is on by default for YouTube, YouTube Music and YouTube Kids. This means that if you're watching a video on YouTube and you leave the YouTube app, the video will keep playing in the background until you pause it. You can turn off background play at any time in your settings.

Learn more about background playHow do I add people to my plan?You can add members through the 'Purchases and memberships' page by clicking on Edit next to 'Family sharing settings'. You can share your YouTube Premium membership with up to one additional household member with a two-person plan and five additional household members with a family plan. Once you've got a plan, you can add members if you're the family manager of your plan. Family members sharing a plan must live in the same household as the family manager and have a Google Account. Each family member gets their own private, personalised account.How can I cancel my membership?Cancel your membership by visiting the Purchases and memberships page. You can rejoin Premium or Premium Lite at any time.Have other questions?Visit the YouTube Help Centre
